# Arkhe Vault - SaaS Business Model & Pricing Strategy

**Model:** Freemium for Good - 2-Year Free Nonprofit Program  
**Target Market:** Domestic violence shelters, safe housing, trauma-informed care organizations  
**Revenue Timeline:** Years 3+ (high conversion expected)

---

## Pricing Tiers

### 🌱 STARTER (Free Forever)
**For:** New nonprofits, pilot programs, organizations with <50 active clients

**Includes:**
- Up to 50 client records
- 2 staff users
- Core client management
- Basic safety flagging
- Email support (48-hour response)
- Community forum access

**Limitations:**
- No AI features (Claude API)
- No advanced reporting
- No API access
- No custom branding

**Price:** **FREE** (no credit card required)

---

### 🚀 GROWTH (Free for 2 Years, then $99/month)
**For:** Established nonprofits with 50-500 active clients

**Includes:**
- Unlimited client records
- 10 staff users
- Full AI features (Claude integration)
  - Client summaries
  - Risk analysis
  - Grant narrative generation
- Advanced reporting & analytics
- Program enrollment tracking
- Safety management dashboard
- Document storage (10GB)
- Priority email support (24-hour response)
- Monthly check-in calls (Year 1)

**2-Year Free Program:**
- **Month 0-24:** $0 (complimentary for qualified nonprofits)
- **Month 25+:** $99/month or $999/year (17% discount)

**Qualification Requirements:**
- 501(c)(3) status or equivalent
- Operating <3 years OR budget <$500K annually
- Letter of intent to use for 24+ months
- Completion of onboarding training

---

### 🏢 ENTERPRISE (Free for 2 Years, then $299/month)
**For:** Large organizations, multi-site operations, 500+ active clients

**Includes:**
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- Unlimited staff users
- Multi-location management
- API access (webhooks, integrations)
- Custom branding (colors, logo)
- Document storage (100GB)
- Advanced security features
  - SSO/SAML integration
  - Custom data retention policies
  - Dedicated security review
- Phone support (business hours)
- Quarterly business reviews
- Dedicated account manager (Year 3+)

**2-Year Free Program:**
- **Month 0-24:** $0 (complimentary for qualified nonprofits)
- **Month 25+:** $299/month or $2,990/year (17% discount)

## The 2-Year Free Program Details

### Why 2 Years?

**Nonprofit Funding Cycle:**
- Year 1: Startup, piloting, no funding
- Year 2: Proving impact, seeking grants
- Year 3+: Operational funding secured, can afford tools

**Our Conversion Strategy:**
- Month 18: "Success check-in" - document their wins
- Month 20: "Transition planning" - discuss budget for Year 3
- Month 22: "Early bird offer" - 25% discount if they commit early
- Month 24: "Welcome to the family" - seamless transition to paid

### What's Included in Free Period:

✅ **Full software access** (no feature restrictions for their tier)  
✅ **Implementation support** (setup, data migration, training)  
✅ **Ongoing support** (help desk, documentation, community)  
✅ **Product updates** (all new features during free period)  
✅ **Data ownership** (they can export anytime, no lock-in)  

### What's NOT Included (Paid Services):

❌ **Custom development** (special features just for them)  
❌ **On-site training** (virtual only; travel expenses extra)  
❌ **Data migration from legacy systems** (DIY or paid service)  
❌ **Integrations** (API access only in Enterprise)

## Revenue Projections

### Conservative Scenario (Year 3)

**Assumptions:**
- 200 organizations on 2-Year Free program by end of Year 2
- 70% convert to paid (140 organizations)
- 80% choose Growth ($99/mo), 20% choose Enterprise ($299/mo)

**Math:**
- 112 orgs × $99/mo × 12 months = $132,816
- 28 orgs × $299/mo × 12 months = $100,464
- **Year 3 ARR: $233,280**

### Optimistic Scenario (Year 3)

**Assumptions:**
- 400 organizations on 2-Year Free program
- 80% convert to paid (320 organizations)
- 70% Growth, 30% Enterprise

**Math:**
- 224 orgs × $99/mo × 12 = $266,112
- 96 orgs × $299/mo × 12 = $344,448
- **Year 3 ARR: $610,560**

### Break-Even Analysis

**Costs to Serve (per organization):**
- Cloud hosting: $10/month
- Support time: $20/month (amortized)
- AI API costs: $15/month (if using AI features)
- **Total: ~$45/month per org**

**Break-even:** Growth tier at $99/mo covers costs with healthy margin after Month 24.

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Low Conversion After 2 Years

**Mitigation:**
- Monthly engagement tracking (are they using it?)
- Proactive outreach at Month 18 (not waiting until Month 24)
- "Success planning" workshops to help them budget
- Payment plans ($83/month instead of $99)
- "Grandfather" pricing (lock in early bird rate forever)

**Fallback:** If <50% convert, pivot to:
- Freemium model (free tier + paid upgrades)
- Grant-funded model (find foundation to subsidize)
- B2B model (sell to government agencies who fund nonprofits)

---

### Risk: High Support Costs

**Mitigation:**
- Self-service knowledge base
- Community forum (peer support)
- Group onboarding (webinars vs 1:1)
- AI chatbot for common questions
- "Champion" program (power users help others)

**Target:** Keep support cost <$20/org/month

---

### Risk: AI API Costs Spiral

**Mitigation:**
- Rate limiting (already implemented)
- Usage tiers (500 AI calls/month free, then $0.01/call)
- Caching common summaries
- "AI budget" feature (orgs set monthly limits)
- Optimize prompts for efficiency

## Legal & Financial Structure

### Recommended Entity Structure:

**Option 1: For-Profit with Social Mission (B-Corp)**
- C-Corp or LLC
- B-Corp certification (legally binds to social mission)
- Can take investment, sell equity
- Tax write-off for "donated" software

**Option 2: Hybrid Nonprofit/For-Profit**
- 501(c)(3) foundation owns for-profit subsidiary
- Software development in for-profit
- Grants and donations to foundation
- Foundation subsidizes software for neediest orgs

**Option 3: Pure For-Profit (Corporate Social Responsibility)**
- Standard C-Corp
- CSR program: "2 years free for nonprofits"
- Tax deduction for charitable contribution
- Easiest structure, clearest to investors

**Recommendation:** Start with Option 3 (simple), transition to Option 1 (B-Corp) when raising investment.
