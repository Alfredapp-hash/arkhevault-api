## Common Questions

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: 24-month commitment during free period. After that, month-to-month or annual.

**Q: Can I upgrade during the 2 years?**
A: Yes. Your free period applies to your tier at Month 24.

**Q: What if we grow beyond our tier?**
A: We don't hard-cap during free period. We'll help you choose the right tier at conversion.

**Q: Do we own our data?**
A: Absolutely. Export anytime. We delete 60 days after you leave.
