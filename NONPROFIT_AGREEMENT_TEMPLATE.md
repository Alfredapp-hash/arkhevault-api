## 1. PURPOSE OF THIS AGREEMENT

Arkhe Vault ("Provider") offers qualifying nonprofit organizations complimentary access to its case management software platform for a period of twenty-four (24) months ("Free Period"). This agreement establishes the terms, conditions, and expectations for participation in the 2-Year Free Nonprofit Program.

**Provider Mission:** To empower organizations serving vulnerable populations with enterprise-grade technology, regardless of budget constraints.

---

## 2. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

To qualify for the 2-Year Free Program, Organization must meet ALL of the following criteria:

☐ **501(c)(3) Status:** Current 501(c)(3) determination letter from IRS (or equivalent nonprofit status)  
☐ **Mission Alignment:** Primary mission includes one or more of the following:
   - Domestic violence prevention or survivor support
   - Safe housing or homelessness services
   - Trauma-informed care
   - Mental health services
   - Substance abuse recovery
   - At-risk youth support
   
☐ **Operational Status:** EITHER:
   - Operating for less than 3 years (start-up nonprofit), OR
   - Annual operating budget under $500,000, OR
   - Demonstrated financial hardship
   
☐ **Commitment:** Agreement to actively use the platform for minimum 24 months  
☐ **Data Consent:** Willingness to share anonymized usage data to improve the platform  
☐ **Feedback:** Participation in quarterly feedback sessions (30 minutes, virtual)

The following services require separate payment or are not available during Free Period:

❌ **Custom Development:** Special features built specifically for Organization  
❌ **On-Site Training:** In-person training (virtual only included)  
❌ **Legacy Data Migration:** Transferring data from previous systems  
  - DIY migration: Free (we provide tools)
  - Assisted migration: $500-$2,000 (quote provided)
❌ **API Access:** Only included in Enterprise tier  
❌ **Custom Integrations:** Connecting to external systems  
❌ **Excess Storage:** Beyond tier limits (additional $0.10/GB/month)  
❌ **Priority Support:** Phone support, <4 hour response (Enterprise only)

## 5. ORGANIZATION COMMITMENTS

During the Free Period, Organization agrees to:

### A. Active Usage
- Maintain at least one active user login per month
- Use platform for core case management functions
- Not resell, sublicense, or transfer access
- Notify Provider of extended inactivity (>60 days)

### B. Data Accuracy
- Maintain accurate organization information
- Promptly update contact and billing information
- Ensure all staff users have valid email addresses

### C. Feedback & Improvement
- Participate in quarterly 30-minute feedback sessions
- Respond to annual impact survey
- Allow use of anonymized aggregate data for case studies
- Optional: Provide testimonial or case study (with client permission)

### D. Compliance
- Use platform in compliance with all applicable laws
- Maintain required nonprofit status throughout Free Period
- Notify Provider within 30 days if nonprofit status changes
- Ensure staff complete required training on platform use

## 7. DATA OWNERSHIP & EXPORT

### Ownership:
- **Organization retains full ownership** of all client data entered
- Provider claims no rights to Organization's data
- Provider may use anonymized aggregate data for platform improvement

### Export Rights:
- Organization may export data **at any time** during Free Period
- Export formats: CSV, JSON, PDF reports
- No fees for data export
- Provider will provide export assistance if technical issues arise

### Post-Termination:
- 30-day window after Free Period ends for data export
- After 60 days, data permanently deleted from Provider servers
- Provider may retain anonymized usage analytics (no PII)

### Uptime Commitment:
- **Target:** 99.5% uptime (excluding scheduled maintenance)
- **Scheduled Maintenance:** Announced 7 days in advance, during off-hours
- **Emergency Maintenance:** As needed, announced via email/status page

### Support Response Times:
| Issue Type | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Critical (system down) | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| High (major feature broken) | 24 hours | 72 hours |
| Medium (partial issue) | 48 hours | 5 business days |
| Low (questions, requests) | 48 hours | Best effort |

**Note:** During Free Period, support is email-only (Growth tier). Phone support available for Enterprise tier.

### Limitations:
- SLA applies to cloud-hosted service only
- Excludes issues caused by Organization's internet connection
- Excludes third-party integrations (API services)
- Force majeure events excluded

### Termination by Organization:
Organization may terminate this agreement at any time with **30 days written notice**.
- No termination fees
- Pro-rated refunds if applicable (paid services only)
- Data export assistance provided

### Termination by Provider:
Provider may terminate this agreement if:
- Organization ceases to meet eligibility requirements
- Organization violates terms (reselling, illegal use, etc.)
- Organization is inactive for >90 days without communication
- Organization fails to complete required training within 60 days of signup

**Termination Process:**
- 30-day notice provided (except for illegal use)
- Data export window provided
- No penalties for Organization

Either party may terminate immediately by mutual written agreement.
